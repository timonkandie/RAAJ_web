Apex Coffee

Packaging & Merchandise

F&B

The Challenge

Apex Coffee was entering a highly saturated local cafe market. They needed packaging bags that communicated artisanal quality while maintaining a strong, recognizable shelf presence across 10 different roast varieties.

The Solution

We created a unified illustration system where each coffee origin featured a custom-drawn geometric pattern. Printed on matte black bags with metallic foil stamping, the packaging became highly Instagrammable and instantly iconic.
