Our Story

Founded in the vibrant heart of Nairobi, RAAJ Studios was born out of a simple conviction: exceptional graphic design is strategic communication.

We saw too many amazing local businesses struggling to communicate their value because their visual identity didn't reflect their quality. We set out to change that. Today, we combine artistic mastery with deep brand strategy to deliver logos, posters, packaging, and marketing collateral that drive real business growth.

Our team is passionate about color, typography, and visual storytelling. We don't just make things look pretty; we solve business problems through design.
